Instructions & notes

Auto-populate coverage: Lookups use the free UPC Item DB API. Works well for retail/commercial chemical products. Lab-grade chemicals from Sigma-Aldrich, Fisher, or VWR may not be in the public database — fill those manually and use the SDS links on the left.
CAS number & container size are almost never encoded in standard barcodes. You will need to enter these manually, or find them on the product label or SDS sheet.
Exporting: Click “Export CSV (Excel)” to download a .csv file. Open it directly in Excel, or copy the rows into your Chemical_Inventory_Tracker.xlsx spreadsheet.
OSHA requirement (29 CFR 1910.1200): SDS sheets must be readily accessible to employees during their shift. Keep printed copies in a binder near chemical storage areas in addition to this digital record.
